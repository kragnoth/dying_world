# Gearwright's Hollow

*The world ended slowly. Most of it is still standing.*

Gearwright's Hollow is a semi-sandbox survival pack for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**, set in a low-magic medieval world that has quietly come apart. Villages stand empty. Chapels have fallen in, mines are flooded and roads lead to nothing. Somewhere in all that, people once built machines that worked a little too well.

You arrive with nothing and rebuild. Along the way you'll run Create engines, Immersive Engineering power grids and blood rituals, and you'll start to see what the old gearwrights saw.

---

## The world

- **A lived-in ruin.** Custom-built structures are scattered lightly through the land: abandoned hamlets and villages, a ruined chapel, plague houses, wayside shrines, wells, collapsed mineheads, an industrial mineshaft and a lighthouse. Each was built by hand and decays the way real buildings do, not as random holes.
- **People who stayed.** Scavenger outposts and strongholds, a tinker's workshop, a researcher's cabin, and research gates into the Nether and the End.
- **Sparser, older land.** Fewer trees, more weather, with biomes from Regions Unexplored and the vanilla backports.

## The vision

Look closely and the world isn't quite what it seems. **Wasteland Vision** lays a second reality over everything you see: the **Occult Foundry**, a soot-and-tar version of the world haunted by things that are only there when you look at them. It changes what you see, not the world itself, and it matters more the deeper you go.

## Progression

**266 quests across 15 chapters** guide you without fencing you in:

| Foundations | Industry | Arcana | Endgame |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awakening · Hearth & Harvest · Seed and Sprocket · Wilds & Ruins · Marks of the Maker · Into the Depths | Gears and Steam · Coke and Current · Sparks and Sails · Crates and Conduits · Ender Engineering | Blood and Ritual · Tar and Spirits | The Incursion · Master of the Hollow |

Quests pay out in skill points, gear and lore, never in shortcuts.

## Systems

- **The Skill Wheel.** One large Path of Exile-style passive tree (Pufferfish's Skills) with combat, survival, exploration and industry sectors. Its keystones grant real abilities through **Kragnoth's Skill Perks**. You can specialise deeply or spread out, and every capstone is reachable.
- **Hallmarked gear.** Everything you craft or loot rolls a quality and a grade. Gear can be tempered, reforged and inlaid, there are named uniques with their own stories, and some sets have bonuses worth chasing.
- **Mob Rules.** Monsters roll affixes, so some are tougher than others. *Elite* and *Champion* mobs can be swift, vampiric, frostbound, splitting, warded, quaking and more, and they're worth killing.
- **Industry.** Create and a deep bench of addons (Aeronautics, New Age, Enchantment Industry, Connected, Deco, Bits 'n' Bobs, Trading Floor and others), plus Immersive Engineering, Immersive Petroleum, Ender IO and Super Factory Manager. Ores double through a unified processing chain.
- **Arcana.** Neo Vitae blood magic, and **Tarbound**: machine spirits bound in black tar, a storage-and-automation path of its own.
- **Hearth and harvest.** Farmer's Delight with its family of add-ons, and **Cog & Cultivar** crop breeding.
- **Getting around.** Waystones, airships, jetpacks and backpacks, with FTB Chunks for claiming land and sharing it with your team.

## The Hollow (light spoilers)

Every road in the pack eventually leads to the Hollow. Something is waiting there, and it's wearing your face.

What you take from it, and what it takes from you, is the endgame. Creative-tier machines exist, but you can only reach them by paying for them.

## Requirements

- **Minecraft 1.21.1**, **NeoForge 21.1.262**, **Java 21**
- **Memory:** 8 GB allocated recommended (6 GB minimum). The pack has around 275 mods.
- Shaders are optional. Iris and Sodium are included.

## Multiplayer

The pack works in multiplayer and keeps its progression per player: the Hollow, the vision and the skill wheel all track each player separately. To run a dedicated server, use the server files if they're attached to this release. Otherwise use the pack's mods minus the client-only ones; the server will refuse to start and name each client-only mod that needs removing.
